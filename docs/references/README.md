applies to ClaimBranch. They inform decisions but do not become product truth on
their own.

- [Documentation-system research](documentation-system-research.md) — official
  agent-instruction, docs-as-code, information architecture, planning, and
  enforcement sources used for this repository structure.
- [Research-agent runtime, graph, and local-serving references](research-agent-runtime-and-graph.md)
  — OpenClaw, GraphRAG, and llama.cpp patterns plus their bounded applicability.
- [Native-Windows trust and recovery references](windows-local-trust-and-recovery.md)
  — primary isolation, loopback-browser, file-replacement, and SQLite sources
  that the platform spikes must verify.
- [Notion MCP coding-journal references](notion-mcp-coding-journal.md)
  — official Notion and OpenAI sources for OAuth, permissions, tools, hooks,
  and the bounded contributor-recording design.
