# ADR 0018: Add optional AI to the personal research workspace

## Context

The manual personal alpha is complete under
[ADR 0017](0017-personal-terminal-alpha.md), without native keys or application
encryption. On 2026-10-09 the user explicitly selected adding optional AI to
that personal app instead of waiting for the protected product. The
[four-unit follow-up](../../plans/active/2026-08-04-claimbranch-v1-and-gpu-systems.md#optional-ai-follow-up-implementation-plan)
and [interaction contract](../../designs/2026-09-22-episode-review.md#typed-proposal-and-return-contract)
own implementation and remaining connections. Scope approval is not provider
selection, permission to transmit a project's content or completed validation.

## Decision drivers

- Help form a judgment before it is complete, without another narrative.
- Retain AI contribution to individual claims and relationships through editing
  and return, including under a human-written judgment.
- Keep manual work useful with AI disabled and the completed alpha available.
- Deliver feedback without making native custody a personal-AI prerequisite.
- Keep explanation choice, provenance and protected authority distinct.

## Options considered

- Wait for protected B09/G2 before all live AI: retains the protected sequence,
  but delays the chosen personal workflow; not selected for this continuation.
- Let an unrestricted agent edit personal files directly: bypasses application
  review and origin preservation; rejected.
- Add explicit scoped proposals, selective human incorporation and durable
  origin to the ordinary personal workspace: selected. Its protection remains
  weaker than the future signed protected product.

## Decision

The next personal version may offer optional AI critique, interpretations,
claims, scientific relationships and unrun experiment suggestions using selected
recorded context and an explicitly included unfinished judgment. AI remains off
by default; no background request or automatic scientific incorporation occurs.

The person compares, selects and edits proposed items, then separately records
the chosen personal consequence with their original reason. Retain complete
selected/required item origins and inherited judgment origin through edits,
corrections, history refresh, restart and backup/restore. AI cannot confirm or
correct evidence, make a scientific decision, write a manuscript, execute tools,
grade comprehension or close protected debt.

Keep ordinary owner-private files outside Git. No device key, signed receipt,
native enrollment or new encryption requirement is added. Personal recording
states human intent and confers no protected accepted authority or protection
against unrestricted same-user programs. Existing encrypted/native work remains
unchanged and cannot gain or lose authority through this route.

Explanation review is separate from AI origin. Quiet help and staging impose no
quiz. Consequential personal review reuses the existing reason and contextual
basis, retaining partial explanation and an explicit human review/leave-for-later
choice. Recorded review does not certify understanding or create accepted debt.

Before live dispatch, qualify one explicitly chosen actual provider under
[ADR 0009](0009-external-inference-and-local-authority.md): verify no exposed
research-write/key/human-operation capability through actual paths/mounts,
credentials, IPC and APIs, and obtain exact one-shot outbound review. Hosted or
separate-host labels, compatible JSON, localhost and connector isolation alone
are insufficient. An unrestricted same-user inference server remains unsupported
without demonstrated protection. Freeze credentials, retention, deadlines and
request budget at that connection; this ADR selects no endpoint or account.

## Consequences

Portable typed preparation and offline rehearsals proceed now. Live enablement
waits for complete item origin/return, contextual interaction and actual provider/
egress checks. The current public manual alpha stays AI-off until those pass;
this decision does not falsely enable unfinished features or reopen its S count.

Personal files and backups remain unencrypted. Model/provider loss preserves
manual work and earlier origin. Failed saves cannot discard live writing or
truncate contributions. The full protected B11 path keeps its B09/G2 gates;
this earlier personal continuation neither completes nor weakens that program.

## Validation

Use the existing [follow-up acceptance cases](../../validation/cases/saturation.md#131-optional-ai-follow-up-acceptance-proposal).
Check selected input, closed typed ingress, malicious/unselected references,
atomic selective staging, complete origin under a manual judgment and fresh-
process/backup return. Rehearse the existing terminal on new synthetic content
before live use; then observe one researcher-authored novel task with explicit
source/outbound choice. Verify manual fallback, cancellation and late arrivals.
Portable fixtures cannot prove provider protection, useful critique or human
understanding. No public provider support is claimed before actual evidence.

## Supersession

Partially supersedes [ADR 0017](0017-personal-terminal-alpha.md)'s deferral of
live AI from the personal-release scope. Its completed first
manual release, ordinary storage, weaker authority, evidence, terminal and
manuscript boundaries remain. ADRs 0003/0005/0006/0009 and the protected B11
dependency order remain in force; no historical accepted decision is rewritten.
