# Candidate MVP release scope

This is a conditional product hypothesis, not the current implementation plan.
The smaller [validation prototype](validation-prototype.md) must first finish
with a VALIDATED live-paper outcome. ClaimBranch has no usable application or
settled stack today.

## 1. Entry gate

The candidate MVP may be planned only when all of these remain true:

- three prospective episodes completed voluntarily under the frozen V0 rule;
- accepted-state, egress, human-authority, file-safety, and recovery rules
  passed without catastrophe;
- trace completeness was 100%;
- median ClaimBranch active time was no more than 10% above the
  Markdown/checklist baseline;
- at least one material omission was surfaced and attested before the final
  decision;
- the clean-checkout and unfamiliar-user journeys still pass;
- export, copy-on-write migration, restore, and both H0 goldens still verify;
  and
- the researcher wants to continue using ClaimBranch on real work.

If V0 is inconclusive, not validated, or fails to demonstrate value, only the
failed finite contract or workflow may be repaired and re-evaluated. This
candidate list does not authorize adjacent platform work.

## 2. Candidate outcome

The candidate MVP is a dependable daily local assistant for the repeated
result-to-paper loop:

    capture a real result
      -> confirm evidence
      -> inspect competing reasoning
      -> request optional AI critique
      -> authorize selected scientific change
      -> understand or visibly defer explanation review
      -> review the manuscript consequence
      -> apply, compile, recover, and close debt

The researcher remains the manuscript author and scientific authority. AI stays
replaceable and proposal-only. Graph and execution detail remain available for
inspection but do not dominate the daily surface.

## 3. Candidate capabilities

Capabilities advance independently through observed triggers; VALIDATED V0 does
not mean build all of them at once.

### 3.1 Repeated real-paper operation

- multiple bounded ResearchEpisodes in one project;
- compatible real result intake through manual templates and the smallest
  proven adapter;
- current and historical evidence-watermark views;
- focused Claim, reasoning, contribution, debt, and manuscript inspection;
- robust status, diagnosis, export, migration, and recovery;
- one calm Episode Review and small Review Pending surface; and
- a generated, version-matched public CLI and user documentation.

Generalized import mapping enters only when two independent result formats
cannot be captured without repeated manual re-entry.

### 3.2 Reasoning growth

- additional typed records or relationships only when real episodes attest that
  the finite contract omitted a material concept;
- additional branch/merge shapes only after two independent episodes require
  the same out-of-F0 shape;
- correction paths that append history and make supersession legible; and
- focused context navigation before any full graph canvas.

A universal ontology, generic event-sourcing framework, general three-way
semantic merge, automatic conflict solver, or arbitrary extension bag is not a
default MVP capability.

### 3.3 Manuscript growth

- more marked blocks or files only after the single-anchor saga remains safe;
- exact side-by-side diffs, pinned compile evidence, and human closure for every
  affected debt;
- bounded anchor repair and correction flows; and
- optional Git review/materialization only after the researcher explicitly
  requests it.

Repository-wide auto-editing, arbitrary LaTeX AST rewriting, automatic Git
promotion, and claims of graph/filesystem atomicity remain out.

### 3.4 AI and serving growth

- provider-neutral Proposal envelopes and complete Contributions;
- one or more quality-qualified providers selected per task and privacy policy;
- visible latency, schema, context, and disposition evidence;
- a local endpoint as a default candidate only after it passes frozen quality
  and security thresholds; and
- an educational serving track in a separate plan/repository when the user wants
  deeper model, quantization, or hardware study.

Broader serving benchmarks require at least 30 frozen cases and the numeric
privacy/latency trigger in the active ExecPlan. ClaimBranch does not become a
model manager merely because local inference is useful.

### 3.5 Runtime integration

An OpenClaw or MCP adapter is considered only when at least two manual transfer
events occur in each of at least two episodes and a least-privilege capability
test denies every human-only command and raw write.

The adapter may inspect bounded context and create Proposals. It cannot confirm
evidence, authorize a review, merge reasoning, apply a patch, close debt, export
arbitrary data, or hold the accepted store. OpenClaw remains the general
assistant/runtime; ClaimBranch remains the scientific authority kernel.

### 3.6 Retrieval and storage growth

Typed neighborhood traversal plus full-text search remains the baseline.
Embeddings or GraphRAG require a frozen corpus of at least 30 retrieval
questions and recall below 90% after two bounded tuning attempts. A graph-native
store requires either a correctness invariant SQLite cannot implement or a
measured design budget missed by more than 20% after two profiled attempts.

Derived extraction, similarity, community, and summary records stay disposable
and never become accepted scientific edges implicitly.

### 3.7 UI growth

The quiet editorial Episode Review remains primary. Broader Inbox/context
correction gets one focused iteration before a graph canvas or dashboard is
considered. A broader visual surface requires at least five recorded navigation
failures across at least three qualifying episodes after that iteration.

Chat may become contextual capture or query, never the source of truth or
default home. Accessibility gates remain part of every expansion.

## 4. Candidate release criteria

A future MVP plan must add exact numeric gates, but it cannot weaken these:

1. accepted evidence remains global, append-only, and visible across reasoning
   branches;
2. every accepted AI-influenced change retains complete Contribution,
   ReviewBundle, receipt, Decision, and debt state;
3. human-only actions are denied outside the trusted foreground capability;
4. AI-off operation covers the complete accepted repository and manuscript
   recovery path;
5. every real manuscript write either verifies or proves exact restore/visible
   hard stop;
6. upgrade and restore cannot silently change authority;
7. the daily path remains faster or materially more complete than the baseline;
8. onboarding, errors, and recovery succeed for an unfamiliar supported user;
9. local diagnostics and measurements disclose no data automatically; and
10. product value is supported by repeated live use, not only fixtures or AI
    evaluations.

## 5. Still not the MVP by default

- collaboration, multi-user authority, SaaS, remote access, mobile, or direct
  Overleaf control;
- experiment execution/scheduling or artifact/checkpoint hosting;
- full literature/PDF analysis or automatic related-work writing;
- autonomous scientific decisions or manuscript edits;
- general Research OS, OpenClaw clone, plugin/provider marketplace, or channel
  platform;
- model download/quantization/supervision/router;
- universal ontology, general semantic VCS, or full graph canvas;
- public installer and auto-update before the release plan verifies migration,
  support, and rollback; and
- a v1.0 label before the future release's own live acceptance passes.

## 6. Scope-control rule

Every expansion must name:

- the observed episode evidence and numeric trigger;
- the smallest capability that resolves it;
- new authority, privacy, recovery, migration, and usability risks;
- a bounded fixture and live acceptance oracle; and
- what remains explicitly out of scope.

Convenience, trendiness, hardware ownership, or “graph engineering” alone is
not admission evidence.
